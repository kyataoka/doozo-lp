What Doozo Does

Only the photos you chose

Choose what to show, tap Hand Over, and pass your iPhone. However they swipe, they move only among the photos you chose. Zoom, videos, and Live Photos work too.

Only you can close it (Doozo Plus)

With Doozo Plus, lock with Face ID before you hand it over. While locked, it can’t be closed until you tap the lock and authenticate.

Show them again from History (Doozo Plus)

With Doozo Plus, what you showed stays in History, ready to show again. Your photos and history stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us.

What Doozo Can’t Prevent

Doozo keeps people from moving on to photos you didn’t choose within the app. It can’t stop notifications or going to the Home Screen or other apps. If that matters, use Guided Access on your iPhone too; the steps are in the app’s Settings under How to Use.
