How it works

Choose, then hand it over.

No more worrying that they’ll swipe past what you meant to show.

  1. 1

    Choose photos

    Pick only what you want to show, in the photo picker you already know.

  2. 2

    Choose how, tap Hand Over

    Let them swipe freely, or show one at a time. Arrange or remove photos here too.

  3. 3

    Hand it over

    However they swipe, they move only among the photos you chose.

Only what you chose

They can’t go past your photos

Swiping, tapping the thumbnails, or opening the grid, they only ever see the photos you chose. Nothing next to them in your library, and no screenshot you just took, can turn up.

Zoom, videos, and Live Photos work as usual, and photos are shown at their original quality.

Face ID lock

Only you can close itPLUS

Lock with Face ID before you hand it over. While locked, the close button disappears, and it can’t be closed until you tap the lock and authenticate.

Turn on Lock When Handing Over, and it locks every time you hand it over.

One at a time

Show one at a timePLUS

Show photos in the order you tell the story, with what comes next kept hidden. Only the photos already shown line up below, so nobody can peek ahead.

History

Show them again from HistoryPLUS

What you showed stays in History. Name it, pin it, or add photos, so “Can I see those trip photos again?” takes a single tap.

Your photos and history stay on your iPhone and are never sent to us.

Privacy

Your photos never leave your iPhone.

An app for showing photos has to be careful with them above all.

No account

No email address, no sign-in. Install it and start using it.

Photos are never sent

The photos you choose and your history are stored only on your iPhone. We never receive your photos or which ones you chose.

You decide on tracking

Ads are personalized only if you choose Allow when iOS asks about tracking. Everything works the same if you don’t, and paid plans don’t load ads at all.

What Doozo can’t prevent

Doozo keeps people from moving on to photos you didn’t choose within the app. It can’t stop notifications or going to the Home Screen or other apps. If that matters, use Guided Access on your iPhone too; the steps are in the app’s Settings under How to Use.
